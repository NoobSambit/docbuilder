# Akshara · The living manuscript

Ancient authorship translated into a warm editorial AI workspace.

- Palette: pale warm stone #F2F0EA, soft charcoal #282B2A, subtle clay accent #8B6954.
- Type: one refined modern sans-serif family similar in proportions to a contemporary humanist grotesk; medium 48–56px marketing headings, 14px controls, calm 28px application titles; no huge bold serif.
- Art: a sophisticated near-monochrome editorial artwork: layers of ancient palm-leaf manuscript edges and a soft sandstone river gorge at dawn, photographed with matte archival texture. In the distant right-side rock face, a very small fine charcoal mural of Vyasa dictating to a scribe quietly connects this to Mahabharata authorship. Figures occupy less than ten percent of the image and are not foreground deity portraits. Pale sand, natural grain, soft raking light, calm negative space, no golden god-ray spectacle, no dense library wall, no ornate devotional statues.
- Product connection: The scribe turns a thought into a lasting manuscript.
- Hero composition: Pale manuscript textures and a tiny epic scribe mural; restrained sans-serif copy and neutral actions.
- Implementation consideration: The scribe must remain a background artwork; miniature carving detail may be reduced on mobile.
