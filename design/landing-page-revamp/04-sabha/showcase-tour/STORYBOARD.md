# Sabha — guided scroll showcase

Scope: new raster mockups for the pinned container tour only. No runtime changes, hero rebuild, lower-section redesign or other-theme generation. These references supersede the single refinement-only showcase as the design for this tour.

## Visual thesis

A compact, pearl-and-plum product tour makes each action and its result visible inside one continuous workspace. Clear stage narration and deliberate focus replace tiny changing utility tabs beside an unchanged document.

The first Brief candidate was rejected by the user for sparse, oversized form/outline presentation. Its dimensions were explicitly accepted. The next pass keeps that aspect ratio but uses a compact project bar, compact chapter track, a shorter narration band, and nearly full-height working panes. Density comes from editable section/subsection structure, source context and relevant inspector content; it does not mean adding unrelated controls or shrinking labels to illegible sizes.

## Four-stage story

| Stage | Visitor takeaway | Visible cause and result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Brief | Set the direction before drafting. | A concrete goal plus audience/tone/length produces five editable outline sections. A finished document is not shown yet. |
| Research | Know what the draft is built on. | Two included source records sit beside emerging draft prose; a selected citation is visibly connected to its source summary. |
| Refine | You decide which changes become your text. | A concise instruction leads to a prominent inline pending diff, with accept/discard and the citation retained. |
| Present | Review the result and take it with you. | A finished portrait document and a separate presentation example visibly support DOCX/other document formats and PPTX. |

An extra accepted-edit frame may be added if it improves the refinement handoff. It belongs to Refine, not a fifth chapter.

## Shared visual grammar

- One laptop viewport per image, 16:9, requested 1920×1080; record actual dimensions. No browser/device frame or contact sheet.
- Stable brand/project bar and four-stage progress track: Brief, Research, Refine, Present. Prior stages show completion; the active stage is unambiguous.
- A compact but clearly readable 26–28px stage headline and one 15–16px explanatory sentence are inside a short narration band. Marketing navigation disappears while pinned. Most of the viewport belongs to useful product content.
- Main product content changes meaningfully across stages. Pane focus may change while the outer shell, project and stage track stay continuous.
- Sabha palette: pearl, ivory, dark plum/aubergine, lavender-gray, dusty mulberry and a restrained brass detail. No new saturated color scheme or mythological artwork inside the tour.
- All software chrome and labels use modern sans serif. Document output alone may use an editorial serif. Useful UI labels are readable, not tiny Times-style text.
- Compact 36–40px controls, fine separators, calm solid surfaces, light shadows. No full-screen bland report sheet, decorative dashboards, nested bulky forms or tutorial-callout clutter.
- At most two local attention cues per stage. A visible relationship between input and result is more useful than many numbered annotations.

## Example continuity

Project: **Clean Energy Outlook**. Goal: **Explain the clean energy transition to leadership.** Audience: **Leadership**. Tone: **Analytical**. Length: **Executive brief**.

Outline: **Executive summary**, **Market landscape**, **Storage and flexibility**, **Risks and considerations**, **Next steps**.

Sources: **Renewables 2025 — IEA** and **Energy Storage — US Department of Energy**. The mockup prose is illustrative, without invented statistics or source quotations.

Research sentence: **Energy storage helps save renewable electricity for times when wind and solar output is lower. [2]**

Suggested shorter sentence: **Storage saves renewable electricity for later use. [2]**

Refinement is pending until accepted; the source marker stays with the sentence. The document/presentation outputs are separate examples, not an instant costless format swap.

## Motion handoff

- Brief → Research: the generated outline becomes navigation; draft prose appears and the source inspector becomes the focal companion.
- Research → Refine: the relevant sentence gains focus, the source summary gives way to the concise instruction and pending edit.
- Refine → Present: accepted text persists into a reviewed document; the output stage shows the separate deck example alongside format-appropriate exports.
- Keep native scroll and the stable outer shell. These still images specify the beats; they do not prove implementation or animation.

## Review gate

Review each actual generated image for: three-second comprehension, action/result clarity, continuity, readable foreground text, correct source/format labels, modern hierarchy, purposeful density, palette consistency and implementable structure. Reject dropped captions, mismatched project data, a finished draft in Brief, a tiny off-to-the-side diff, ambiguous output formats or repeated nearly identical screens. Selection is an agent visual review, not user approval.
