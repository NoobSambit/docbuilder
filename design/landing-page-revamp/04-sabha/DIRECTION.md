# Sabha · The epic assembly

An intricate epic assembly frames a rational compact product composition.

- Palette: limestone #EDE9DF, aubergine #342D3B, plum #634558; saffron and verdigris in art.
- Container surfaces: Aubergine #49334F topbar with ivory text; lavender-gray #DFD7E4 outline; pale blush-paper #F3EBF0 editor; dusty mauve #CBBBCF research rail; plum #634558 controls and dark plum ink. Refined tonal panels, not just plum buttons on white.
- Artwork: An original richly detailed Indian miniature painting of the Mahabharata assembly hall. A cutaway palace with scalloped arches and jali screens reveals seated Pandava princes, an elder scholar and clearly blue Krishna in a peacock crown discussing a manuscript. A scribe records the meeting on palm leaves. Carpets, painted niches, a green reflecting pool and ordered columns create intricate layers. Deliberate architectural geometry and legible figure groups, not a noisy crowd. Matte gouache, fine ink contours, paper grain, lapis and terracotta details. Recognizable mythological narrative, not an empty hotel colonnade. Treat the assembly as a working editorial meeting: Krishna reviews sources, a prince arranges document sections, and the scribe prepares a slide storyboard beside a small modern laptop. No debate about war, weapons or armor.
- Implementation: Retain recognizable figure groups in mobile art crops.
