# Astra · The chariot of intent

An epic chariot painting gives a focused dark product story momentum.

- Palette: midnight navy #111C27, chalk #F1EEE6, muted copper #B77949; lapis and rust in art.
- Artwork: An original Mahabharata oil painting, richly detailed like a restored epic painting. Recognizable blue Krishna with a peacock-feather crown guides a carved chariot; Arjuna with a curved bow stands behind him. Two white horses, an ornate bronze chariot wheel, rust-red silk banners and a deep lapis sky form bold overlapping diagonals. Krishna's calm face, Arjuna's focused profile and plausible horse anatomy must be visible. Aged canvas texture, cinematic natural light, sober epic mood. No violence, neon effects, game armor, particles or tiny generic archer.
- Implementation: Readable light-on-dark chrome; solid light editor paper.
