# Astra · The guided draft

Krishna and Arjuna shape a report in an epic writing studio, beside a midnight-and-copper application.

- Palette: midnight navy #111C27, blue-gray paper #DFE9E8, muted copper #B77949; lapis and rust in art.
- Container surfaces: Midnight #12212C topbar; slate #1E3340 outline with chalk labels; blue-gray #DFE9E8 editor with navy ink; dark steel #223848 research rail and #2F4653 source rows with chalk labels; copper #BD8E59 controls. Toolbar #253F4F. Strong dark-versus-light architecture, not white SaaS panels with a brown button.
- Artwork: An original richly painted Mahabharata-inspired writing studio. Recognizable blue Krishna with a peacock-feather crown sits beside Arjuna at a broad carved desk in a river pavilion. Krishna reviews an open source manuscript and points at an arranged outline of paper sections. Arjuna writes at a slim modern laptop showing a simple document page, with printed report sheets and slide-storyboard thumbnails beside it. Krishna is a guide and Arjuna is the author. Fine natural oil pigment, mature brush detail, indigo river, rust-red textiles and warm lamplight create layered epic imagery tied directly to researching, drafting and presenting. No weapons, bows, chariots, armor, soldiers, battlefield, aggression or game art.
- Implementation: Readable dark chrome; contrasting blue-gray editor paper.
