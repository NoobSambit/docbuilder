# Neel · The painted story garden

A richly painted Krishna garden gives a crisp cobalt-and-paper workspace character.

- Palette: warm white #F5F1E7, indigo #162E57, cobalt #315BA8; lotus pink and leaf green in art.
- Container surfaces: Deep indigo #173C70 topbar with ivory text; soft blue #D7E3ED outline; pale mineral-paper #EEF3E8 editor with indigo ink; muted teal #C6D9DD research rail; cobalt #315BA8 actions. Rich color zoning across large container surfaces, never an all-white shell.
- Artwork: A sophisticated Pichwai-inspired epic authorship garden: clearly blue Krishna with a peacock-feather crown sits at a painted garden desk, reading source notes and arranging report pages with a slim modern writing laptop and a small slide storyboard. Dense kadamba leaves, peacocks, lotus pond, white cows and a scalloped pavilion frame this creative work. Mineral pigments, matte paper grain, precise hand-painted contours, layered flat space and small natural gold details. Krishna is recognizably researching and shaping a document rather than only playing music. No battle or weapons, no generic gradient or mascot.
- Implementation: Separate image crops; no ornament beneath utility text.
