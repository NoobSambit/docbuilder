Use case: ui-mockup.

Product truth: DocBuilder AI creates structured Word documents or PowerPoint presentations. The user chooses a title and output type, asks AI for an outline, generates section content, optionally enables web research, edits and refines individual sections with natural-language instructions, reorders sections, and exports DOCX or PPTX. The existing product has four PPTX themes. Treat the application shown here as a proposed visual redesign of those real capabilities. Do not invent collaborative cursors, upload workflows, PDF export, fake customer logos, revenue metrics, ratings, speed guarantees, confidence scores, or pricing.
Visual quality: exceptionally premium, art-directed, carefully aligned, quiet and editorial. Modest confident sans-serif type, coherent spacing, meaningful image crops, clean flat application controls, hairline dividers, restrained radius, one action accent. Rich artwork and rigorous modern UI must coexist. Everything can be built with ordinary HTML/CSS, bitmap background images and a sticky scroll sequence using opacity and transforms. No impossible holograms, 3D UI perspective, floating shards, elaborate physics, or giant ornamental card grids.
Typography: readable real English, crisp anti-aliased letters, natural kerning, no faux-language, no gibberish. Exact supplied copy must be reproduced verbatim. Where dense document prose would be too small, use restrained light-gray text lines beneath a real heading; never fill the page with tiny noisy text. All marketing copy has high contrast against a calm image area or solid surface.
